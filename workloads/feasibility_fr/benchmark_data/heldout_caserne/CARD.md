# Scenario card: heldout_caserne (held-out)

A former fire station offered for sale by a public owner. **Everything here is invented**: the commune, the address, the parcel, the owner and every document. No real place, person or body is described; any resemblance to one is unintended. Every document says in its first paragraph that the site and the document are fictional.

This scenario was written after the engine was frozen (tag `engine-freeze-m3`), under the rules in `docs/notes/heldout.md`. The engine was never run on it before the single measured run.

## Content

Site: "Ancienne caserne des pompiers, 12 rue des Tanneurs à Saint-Aubin-du-Val". Seller and owner: "Communauté de communes du Val d'Aubin". Parcel BK 57, 3,480 m², zone Ub. The regulation is split by zone, and the files of zones Ua and Uc are in the dataroom.

19 documents, 22 facts, 12 traps: unit 1 (a rent per quarter), contradiction 1 (a brochure against the risk report on flooding), superseded 1 (a lease amendment), distractor 1 (the footprint rule of two other zones), buried 2 (height and setback, deep in the regulation), date logic 2 (an expired certificate, a lease that has ended), missing 2 (no soil study, no pollution study), arithmetic 2 (rent total, floor-area total).

## Wording

The documents use none of the dev wording: other sentences for each fact, other row labels, other titles and frames. For each fact two phrasings were written and one was drawn with the scenario's seed (`benchmark/heldout_style.py`). The questions asked of the systems are worded as in dev.

## What it is not

It does not stand on a real parcel or on public data, unlike the dev scenarios. Its layouts and its boilerplate paragraphs come from the same generator as dev.
